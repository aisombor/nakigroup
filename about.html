About Us

Naki Group

Since 2013, Naki Group has grown from a local real estate and property management firm into an investment firm focused on developing the Indo-China belt. With representation in Singapore and Seattle, the Group invests in our 3 key pillars of property development, realty services, and hospitality management.

As a developer, Naki Group has completed Silvertown Metropolitan, a 111-unit condominium in 2014, Woodland Residences, an 84-unit luxury villa gated community to be completed in 2022, and Agile Sky Residence, a 44-storey condominium to be completed in 2022.

As a property management firm, Naki Group have served Silvertown Metropolitan, Woodland Residences, Agile Showroom, TK Avenue Community Mall and The One Mall. In addition to managing medium to large projects, our property management services extend to those smaller shops who require janitorial, pest control and sanitization services.

As a hospitality services provider, Naki Group has maintained a high quality standards of stay to our tenants. From long stay to short stay, we ensure enjoyable accommodation coupled with services of convenience.

Now as a growing real estate company, Naki Group is determined to reach out to larger domestic and overseas projects. On a larger scale, investors both financial and expertise will play a large role in achieving the goal of higher and faster returns to our partners.

Leadership

VEASNA MEAS

CEO, NAKI GROUP

Veasna Meas is a real estate investor and developer with a track record of building and scaling real estate enterprises across the United States and Southeast Asia. Born in Cambodia and raised in the United States, he earned his Bachelor's degree in Business Administration before launching his entrepreneurial journey. His background spans retail management, banking, and real estate investment, giving him a strong foundation in market dynamics, financial structuring, and operational execution. As founder and managing principal, he oversees $210 million in assets under management.

In 2013, Veasna established Naki Group in Cambodia, transforming it from a startup into a diversified real estate company recognized for delivering innovative solutions across commercial development, asset management, property management, and strategic investments. Under his leadership, Naki Group has successfully executed large-scale commercial projects, optimized portfolio performance for investors, and consistently identified high-growth opportunities in Southeast Asian markets.

Building on Naki Group's success, Veasna expands operations into the US market in 2024 focusing specifically on new home construction. In just one year, this strategic expansion has achieved remarkable traction with 150 homes currently under construction, demonstrating Veasna’s ability to rapidly scale operations and adapt proven business models across diverse global markets.

VEASNA MEAS CEO, NAKI GROUP

SREYTOCH TY

Group Manager

Sreytouch Ty serves as Group Manager of Naki Group, where she provides strategic oversight across all operational facets of the organization. In her leadership role, she manages the company's diverse portfolio of activities, including comprehensive property management services and hotel operations, ensuring seamless execution and operational excellence across all business units.

Ms. Ty recently spearheaded the successful handover of Agile Sky Residence, the company's latest development project, demonstrating her expertise in project management and her ability to deliver complex real estate developments from conception to completion.

Beyond her operational responsibilities, Ms. Ty leads design direction across the portfolio of the company's real estate development arm in the United States, shaping exterior architecture and interior concepts in close collaboration with architects and the development team. She drives layout planning, material and finish selections, and renderings that support entitlements and marketing efforts — bringing a cohesive design vision to each project from concept through completion.

Her multilingual capabilities in English, Khmer, and Chinese enable her to effectively communicate with diverse stakeholders and expand the company's reach across multiple markets. With her comprehensive understanding of the real estate and hospitality sectors, Sreytouch Ty continues to drive growth and operational success through her strategic vision and hands-on management approach across all organizational ventures.
