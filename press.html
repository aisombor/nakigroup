Soho and Naki Group join the love the Kids charity event
January 2020 Phnom Penh, Cambodia CSR & Community

SOHO Diversified Group of Companies and Naki Group donated food, shoes and school supplies to the children of Love The Kids home in Phnom Penh.

In a joint charitable effort to support underprivileged youth and foster community solidarity, team members from SOHO Diversified Group of Companies (including staff from Havaianas, Crocs, and Bonchon) joined forces with Naki Group staff for a heartwarming visit to the Love The Kids home in Phnom Penh.

During the special day, volunteers organized interactive games, shared meals with the children, and distributed essential daily necessities, footwear, and educational materials to help young learners thrive in their schooling.

For media inquiries or community partnership proposals, please contact info@nakigroup.com.

Silvertown Metropolitan offers great year-end sale
December 2020 BKK1, Phnom Penh Property Investment

Naki Group, one of the leading property developers in Cambodia, is set to launch the sale and promotion of units within its Silvertown Metropolitan property.

Towering at 14 floors with 111 condominium units including an expansive 420-square-meter penthouse, Silvertown Metropolitan delivers world-class serviced accommodation in the heart of Phnom Penh's premier BKK1 district.

The year-end promotion provides attractive investment yields, flexible payment terms, and fully furnished suites for local and international property investors looking for prime capital growth and stable rental returns in Cambodia's thriving urban center.

Learn more about available units at silvertowncambodia.com.

Agile Sky Residence Donates 2,000 Helmets for "Keep Cool, Keep Safe" Campaign
November 2019 Phnom Penh, Cambodia Road Safety Campaign

Agile Sky Residence and the Naki Group jointly hosted the "Keep Cool, Keep Safe" campaign to encourage road safety and prevent head injuries.

Demonstrating a strong commitment to corporate social responsibility and community welfare, Agile Sky Residence in partnership with Naki Group donated 2,000 certified safety helmets to primary school students, youth volunteers, and local traffic safety officers throughout Phnom Penh.

The campaign aimed to raise public awareness regarding motorbike safety standards, reduce road accidents, and safeguard vulnerable young commuters and families across the capital city.

Learn more about Agile Sky Residence on Facebook at facebook.com/agileKH/.
