Silvertown Metropolitan

Completed in 2014, Silvertown Metropolitan is a 14-story condominium in the heart of BKK1, Phnom Penh. The building has 111 units, ranging from studios to two- and three-bedroom apartments, and a 420-square-meter penthouse. Residents have access to a gym, a rooftop bar, and a rooftop infinity pool with panoramic views of the city, along with on-site management and services. Set in one of Phnom Penh's most established neighborhoods, it offers a central address within walking distance of local restaurants, cafés, and shops, as well as international franchise outlets, and a short drive from the city's major shopping malls.

Naki Suites @ Silvertown

Naki Suites @ Silvertown is a condo-hotel operated by Naki Hospitality within Silvertown Metropolitan, located in the heart of BKK1, Phnom Penh's most stylish and youthful residential quarter. The area is known for its wide selection of restaurants, trendy bars, and cultural attractions. The property offers modern, spacious suites with full kitchenettes, available for nightly, mid-term, and long-term stays. Guests have access to the building's rooftop infinity pool and bar and its gym. Naki Hospitality manages the suites on behalf of unit owners, combining hotel-style service with the space and comfort of a residence.

Agile Sky Residences

Agile Sky Residence is a 44-story residential tower on Preah Monivong Boulevard in BKK3, one of Phnom Penh's established central districts. Completed in 2022, it was developed as a joint venture between Agile Group Holdings Limited, one of China's top ten developers, and Naki Group. The building has 963 units, from studios to three-bedroom apartments, 39 to 111 square meters, and the 57-square-meter one-bedroom is the most common layout. Interiors are by a renowned Hong Kong designer. Amenities include a sky pool with city views, a sky club and café, a book lounge, a fitness center and yoga area, nine high-speed lifts, security cameras, and 424 parking spaces.
